India's MSME registrations, merchandise trade, and industrial production — with year-on-year and month-on-month change computed from official releases.

Data is fetched from official Government of India sources by a scheduled job and committed to this repository — the page reads that snapshot, so it loads instantly and works even when source portals are down. Trade figures refresh after the Commerce Ministry's monthly release (~15th); IIP after NSO Quick Estimates (~28th). All figures are provisional and subject to official revision. Percentages are computed from stored prior observations, never estimated.
